What is DPIIT Recognition?

DPIIT (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) recognition is an official status granted by the Government of India to startups that meet specific criteria of innovation and scalability.

8 Key Benefits of DPIIT Recognition

Unlock exclusive government benefits and accelerate your startup growth

Tax Exemption

100% income tax exemption for 3 consecutive years under Section 80-IAC

Seed Funding

Grant + Debt funding under Startup India Seed Fund Scheme for your startup growth

IPR Subsidy

Major rebate on patent and trademark registration for your startup

Collateral Free Loans

MUDRA and CGTMSE scheme - Easy credit with no collateral required

R&D Support

Major R&D cost borne by Government of India for approved innovation projects

Investor Access

Priority access to angel investor network and VC connect

Government Tenders

100% EMD exemption and fast-track processing in government tenders

Easy Compliance

Self-certification under labour & environmental laws for 3-5 years
