Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and process

How long does company registration take?

7-10 working days for Private Limited/LLP registration. Express service available in 3 days.

What documents are required for registration?

PAN & Aadhaar cards of directors, address proof, and bank statements. We provide complete document checklist.

Do you provide post-registration support?

Yes! 12 months free compliance support including first ROC filing and GST setup.

Is there any hidden charges?

No hidden charges. All government fees and service charges are clearly mentioned upfront.
