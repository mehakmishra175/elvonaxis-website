New Firm Registration

What is Sole Proprietorship and how long does it take?

Single owner business, minimal compliance. Registration within 24 hours.

What is Partnership Firm registration process?

For 2-20 partners with partnership deed. Takes 3-5 days.

What is LLP and its benefits?

Limited Liability Partnership with corporate benefits. Takes 7-10 days.

What is Private Limited Company registration?

Most popular for startups. Takes 7-10 days. Best for funding and VC investment.

What is One Person Company (OPC)?

Private limited benefits for single owner. Takes 7-10 days.

What documents are required for registration?

PAN, Aadhaar, Photo, Address proof, Rental agreement + NOC from owner.

Difference between LLP and Private Limited Company?

LLP: Lower compliance. Pvt Ltd: Better for funding, higher credibility.

Can foreign nationals register company in India?

Yes! Requires valid passport, visa, DIN. One Indian resident director mandatory.

Startup India Program

What is Startup India Registration?

DPIIT recognition for startups. Benefits: tax exemption, fast-track patent, easy funding.

How much tax can I save with Startup India?

100% income tax exemption on profits for 3 consecutive years.

How to get Seed Funding under Startup India?

Eligible startups get grant + debt funding under Startup India Seed Fund Scheme.

What are the benefits of Startup India Certificate?

Tax exemption, seed funding, fast-track patents, R&D cost support by Government.

Who is eligible for Startup India Registration?

Private Limited or LLP, incorporated within 10 years, annual turnover under limit.

Certifications & Licenses

What is MSME Udyam Registration?

Free registration for small businesses. Benefits: collateral-free loans, priority in govt tenders.

What is ISO Certification?

International quality standard certification. Types: 9001, 14001, 22000, 27001, 13485.

What is FSSAI license?

Mandatory for all food businesses. Basic, State, and Central licenses based on turnover.

What is Trademark Registration?

Legal protection for Brand name, Logo, Tagline, Sound mark, Color combinations.

What is GEM Registration?

Government e-Marketplace for selling to govt departments. No tendering required.

What is IEC Code?

Import Export Code mandatory for import/export of goods or services. Online process.

Compliance & Taxation

What are annual compliance requirements?

ROC filing, Income Tax Return, GST returns, TDS returns, Director KYC.

What are penalties for late compliance?

ROC: ₹100/day, GST: ₹50/day, Income Tax: Penalty applicable.

What is GST and is it mandatory?

Mandatory if turnover exceeds threshold, online selling, or inter-state business.

Difference between GST, TDS, and Income Tax?

GST: Tax on sales, TDS: Tax deducted on payments, Income Tax: Tax on annual profits.

What is NSIC Registration?

National Small Industries Corporation registration. Benefits: Tender fee exemption.
